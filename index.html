AED on the map in the Rochester community

OpenStreetMap lists 1 AED in all of Rochester, and 35 in all of Minnesota. The devices exist — they just aren't written down anywhere the public can reach. Read why that matters.

If someone collapses right now

  1. Call 911. Put the phone on speaker.
  2. Send one specific person to get the AED. Point at them.
  3. Perform CPR. If you aren't trained, the 911 dispatcher can talk you through it — start anyway, and don't wait.
  4. Use the AED as soon as it arrives. It talks you through it.

Based on guidance from the American Heart Association and American Red Cross. See our sources.

The best preparation is getting CPR certified before you ever need it. Certification courses are offered by the American Red Cross, the American Heart Association, and local hospitals. Knowing what to do in advance is what turns a bystander into a responder.

What to get in touch about

Correcting or removing a listing

If an AED on the map is in the wrong place, no longer there, or should not be listed at all, tell us and we will fix or remove it promptly. This includes property owners and managers — if you would rather your AED not appear on the map, just ask and we will take it down. Please include the building name so we can find the record.

Adding an AED

The fastest way is the Add an AED form — it captures the exact location details we need. Email works too if you would rather write it out.

An AED that needs attention

If you notice an AED with an expired battery, missing pads, a locked cabinet, or damage, let us know. We will pass it to whoever manages the building. Please also tell the building's staff directly, since they can act faster than we can.

Partnerships and AED placement

Schools, sports organizations, and community groups interested in working with AEDs for Athletes — including anywhere that needs an AED and doesn't have one — are welcome to reach out.

Press and speaking

For media questions or requests for someone to speak about sudden cardiac arrest awareness, email us and we will connect you with the right person.

What we cannot help with

We are a youth-led education and preparedness organization, not a medical or emergency service. While we provide CPR and AED education, we are not a certifying body — for certification, see the accredited providers listed in our disclaimer. We also cannot give medical advice, service or repair AED units, or confirm in real time whether a particular AED is currently working.

Registering an AED officially

Minnesota law requires owners of public-access AEDs to register with an AED registry within 30 days. Listing an AED with us does not satisfy that. Register with the Minnesota AED Registry or another recognized registry.

Always call 911 first. This site is not a substitute for emergency services, medical advice, or CPR and AED training.

Always call 911 first. This site is not a substitute for emergency services, medical advice, or CPR and AED training.

An independent community project for Rochester, Minnesota, built in partnership with AEDs for Athletes — a youth-led 501(c)(3) fostering a culture of preparedness by bringing CPR and AED readiness into every community. This map is not operated by the organization.

AED locations are gathered from community submissions and public sources and may be inaccurate or out of date. We cannot guarantee any AED is present or in working order. Read the full disclaimer.

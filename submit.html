Add an AED

Know where a defibrillator is? Tell us, and we will confirm it in person before it shows on the map as verified.

What happens after you submit

Your submission is marked community reported and appears in blue on the map. A volunteer then visits the location to confirm the AED is present, accessible, and in service. Only after that does it become a verified record. Nothing you enter here is published as verified automatically.

For example: "Mayo High School — main gym"
